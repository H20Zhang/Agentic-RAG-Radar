# Agentic RAG Research Digests

This directory is the **human reading layer** of Agentic RAG Radar. Daily ingestion stays in canonical paper records; reports are compacted at weekly and monthly granularity.

## Weekly

Weekly reports live under [`weekly/`](weekly/) and use ISO-week names such as `2026-W33.md`.

A weekly report answers: **what changed, which papers matter, where evidence conflicts, and what should be read next?**

_No weekly report has been completed yet._

## Monthly

Monthly reports live under [`monthly/`](monthly/) and use calendar-month names such as `2026-08.md`.

A monthly report answers: **how did the research map change, which design patterns are emerging, how strong is the evidence, and what open problems now matter most?**
