# Agentic RAG Yearly — 2026

> **Coverage:** 2026-01-01 to 2026-08-14 · **Status:** rolling · **Scope note:** this is a year-to-date map over papers currently curated by the radar, not a claim of complete historical coverage before backfill matures.

## Year-to-date thesis

**The durable 2026 shift is from “retriever + agent” toward explicit design of the agent's information environment: where the corpus boundary sits, what evidence operations it exposes, what state persists, what policy controls interaction, and what resources are actually spent.**

A major backfill correction strengthens this thesis. By May–July, DCI, RISE, DR-DCI, RARG, GrepSeek, Critic-R, and harness studies had already separated several layers that the earlier radar chronology underrepresented.

The methodological lesson is now stricter: a serious comparison needs **interface matching, harness matching, adaptive baselines, and realized-resource accounting**, plus a historical prior-art baseline when the control-loop shape is old.

## 1. Retrieval becomes an interaction-space problem

A-RAG exposes explicit keyword/semantic/read actions. DCI makes the stronger move: bypass fixed similarity retrieval and expose raw corpus operations, introducing **retrieval-interface resolution** as a design lens. Its trajectory analysis suggests some gains come after gold documents have already been surfaced, implicating localization/verification rather than recall alone.

Raw DCI immediately reveals a scale frontier. RISE restores a retrieval boundary but makes the retrieved set a persistent interaction space instead of final snippets. DR-DCI makes retrieval an agent-callable workspace-expansion action. RARG carries relevance into the execution of local interaction, prioritizing traversal and match visibility.

The durable progression is:

`top-k evidence delivery → raw high-resolution interaction → bounded/dynamic workspace → relevance-guided interaction`

This weakens the false dichotomy “semantic retrieval versus grep.” Relevance remains useful; the question is whether it is a hard evidence bottleneck, a workspace constructor, or an execution prior.

## 2. Evidence and environment become explicit state

S2G-RAG makes sufficiency/missing-information state explicit. DocNavRAG couples evidence state to document-native navigation. LoongReflect adds rollback/recovery over active reasoning state.

DR-DCI highlights another kind of state: **materialized workspace state persists outside the reasoning context**. Its optional context-reset result suggests the evidence environment can remain useful even when the reasoning trace is discarded. The year-end map should distinguish external workspace durability from internal memory/reasoning recovery rather than grouping both under “memory.”

## 3. Learning moves across multiple control boundaries

Agentic-R changes retriever utility from static similarity toward trajectory-level downstream value. Critic-R uses natural-language introspective feedback to repair retrieval at inference and create retriever supervision. GrepSeek learns the policy over a direct-corpus shell interface. SPARKLE learns retrieval/query/integration control; Graph-R1 learns graph interaction; SAGE learns per-query resource allocation; LoongReflect learns recovery.

Thus “learned Agentic RAG” is not one research object. A paper should identify whether it learned **ranking, query refinement, corpus operations, state recovery, or budget allocation**, and compare against a controller with the same environment/state/action space.

## 4. Harness and evidence delivery become causal variables

Is Grep All You Need? shows that retriever rankings can change materially with the agent harness and how tool output is delivered. Inline grep beats inline vector across its tested LongMemEval-S grid, yet programmatic delivery reverses the ordering on half of the harness-model pairs.

This is not an argument that one harness is “best.” It is evidence that **harness/delivery belongs inside the experimental model**, not in the implementation appendix. The current causal lens becomes:

`substrate × corpus boundary/interface resolution × harness/delivery × state × policy × realized resources × base model × historical baseline`

## 5. Resource budget becomes both an action and an operating point

Know Before You Fetch separates calls, passage volume, and latency. SAGE makes k a learned per-query action. When Should Active RAG Retrieve? makes held-out usage, retrieval harm, and controller-side probe/generation cost explicit.

The DCI lineage adds another resource: **interaction-space size and local tool execution**. A smaller prompt does not imply lower search cost; fewer tool calls do not imply lower wall time; a retrieval boundary can reduce shell I/O while adding index/preprocessing cost.

## 6. Novelty and composition get harder tests

Forgotten History or Test-of-Time? pushes retrieve→verify→reformulate baselines back to classical IR/QA. VAKRA pushes evaluation forward into executable cross-source composition across APIs and documents.

Together they constrain both ends of a novelty claim: show what is genuinely new compared with older control ideas, then show that the new mechanism survives a heterogeneous environment where entity/provenance/policy consistency matters.

## Papers that currently define the 2026 map

**Interaction environment:** A-RAG, DCI, RISE, DR-DCI, RARG, LLM-Wiki, READ.  
**State/recovery:** S2G-RAG, DocNavRAG, LoongReflect.  
**Learning/control:** Agentic-R, Critic-R, GrepSeek, SPARKLE, Graph-R1, SAGE.  
**Evaluation/resources:** Is Grep All You Need?, Know Before You Fetch, When Should Active RAG Retrieve?, Forgotten History or Test-of-Time?, VAKRA.

## What has weakened

**“Agentic = iterative retrieval”** is no longer a meaningful contribution statement: the control loop is older and the 2026 design space is much more specific.

**“Better retriever = better information access”** is also too weak. DCI shows that evidence resolution and available operations can dominate after relevant documents are already reachable.

**“No retriever = more agentic”** is equally weak. RISE, DR-DCI, and RARG show why scalable relevance/bounding can improve direct interaction rather than oppose it.

**“Same model + different retriever = controlled”** is not sufficient if harness/tool-delivery semantics differ.

## Open problems entering the rest of 2026

1. **Adaptive interaction-space boundaries:** learn when/how to expand, contract, or repartition the corpus workspace under explicit latency/I/O/context budgets.
2. **Semantic-symbolic co-design:** use learned relevance to guide exact corpus operations without reintroducing a hard top-k bottleneck.
3. **Layered state semantics:** separate persistent evidence workspace, compacted memory, provenance, and reversible reasoning state.
4. **Interface × harness causal benchmarks:** independently swap corpus interface, evidence-delivery path, controller, and model on the same executable tasks.
5. **Cross-source generalization:** test document-derived interface ideas on web, APIs, SQL, graphs, code, and multimodal data.
6. **Multi-resource operating points:** jointly report index/preprocessing cost, retrieval calls, workspace size, local tool I/O, model tokens, controller compute, wall time, and final quality.
