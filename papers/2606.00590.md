# Critic-R: Improving Agentic Search using Instruction-tuned Retrievers with Natural Language Introspective Feedback

**arXiv:** [2606.00590](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.00590) · **Published:** 2026-05-30 · **Category:** Learning & Optimization · **Importance:** 4/5 · **AI confidence:** High

> **TL;DR.** Critic-R turns the reasoner's natural-language dissatisfaction with evidence into a **retrieval feedback channel**: a critic can repair queries at inference time and convert accepted/rejected refinement attempts into retriever supervision.

## Problem

Search agents often expose retrieval failure in their own next-step reasoning, but the retriever remains a black box. Existing co-training approaches may require gold passages or updates to the reasoner.

## Core idea

Critic-R-Zero speculatively lets the reasoner inspect retrieved evidence, then a separate critic judges whether it satisfies the current information need. Failed evidence triggers a rewritten query and retrieval instruction. Critic-Embed later treats accepted documents as positives and rejected attempts as hard intra-trajectory negatives.

## Agent loop

`retrieve → reason over evidence → critic checks sufficiency → accept or rewrite query/instruction → retrieve again → commit evidence → continue`

## Compared to what

The paper compares with frozen retrieval, Search-R1-style reasoning, and retrieval-learning methods including Agentic-R and CoSearch. Its distinct seam is keeping the reasoner fixed while using process feedback around retrieval.

## Evidence

The paper reports **12.4% relative overall improvement** from Critic-R-Zero, up to **7.5%** from Critic-Embed, and additional gains when both are combined across HotpotQA, 2WikiMultiHopQA, MuSiQue, and Bamboogle. Trajectory positives/negatives support the proposed supervision mechanism.

## Why it matters

This creates an alternative learning path: **agent feedback → retriever improvement** without human passage labels. It is also a retrieval-specific form of inference-time scaling—extra compute is spent only when evidence appears insufficient.

## Limitations / questions

- The critic adds model calls and repeated retrieval attempts; accuracy gains are not a free operating point.
- Supervision is only as trustworthy as the reasoner's introspective trace and the critic's sufficiency judgment.
- Evaluation remains concentrated on multi-hop QA; transfer to heterogeneous tools and real latency budgets needs testing.

**Curator take:** **4/5.** Important because it makes natural-language process feedback a reusable interface between the reasoning trajectory and retrieval model, rather than requiring end-to-end co-training.
