# Skill Following: Evaluating Actual Skill Use in Retrieval-Enabled LLM Agents

*Published 2026-09-01 · Evaluation & Analysis · Importance 4/5 · Full text reviewed · Confidence: medium*

[Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.00549)

> **TL;DR.** The paper exposes a measurement failure: **retrieval coverage and realized retrieval utility are not the same quantity**. For Gemini-2.5-Flash-lite, conventional aggregate lift is positive on three MBPP+ partitions while the same-task Retrieval-conditional Actual-use Effect (RAE) is negative. This supports a cleaner evaluation coordinate, not a claim that retrieval itself is harmful.

## Research question

When retrieval is optional, how much of an apparent gain comes from **which tasks the agent chooses to retrieve on**, and how much comes from the retrieved evidence changing the outcome of those same tasks?

## Mechanism

The skill-enabled agent can call `search_skills`; BM25 searches a small procedural-skill library and returns the top three full skill texts, with at most three calls. A skill-disabled counterpart removes that tool. The evaluation keeps task, model, decoding, and seed paired, then reports three different objects: conventional aggregate retrieved-vs-skipped lift inside skill-enabled runs; Overall Skill-Access Effect (OAE) over all paired tasks; and RAE over the subset where the skill-enabled run actually retrieved.

## Research delta

The closest baseline is not another retrieval algorithm but the **usual aggregate comparison itself**. Aggregate lift compares two self-selected task subsets and can therefore reward a good retrieval trigger even when retrieved content fails to help the tasks on which it was used. OAE removes that task-composition mismatch over the full paired set; RAE focuses the pairwise comparison on actual retrieval invocations.

## Open question

RAE is still conditioned on the **post-treatment retrieval subset** chosen by skill-enabled execution, so it is not an unbiased average treatment effect over a pre-treatment population. Skill-enabled and skill-disabled runs also differ in tool availability. A stronger causal design would keep the interface fixed and independently randomize/force invocation, returned content, and content visibility, while reporting token, tool-call, latency, and dollar budgets.

## Where it fits

`retrieval policy → retrieval invocation → returned skill → context uptake → same-task outcome`.

`early_signal`: **retrieval-invoked actual use** is a useful evaluation coordinate, but one procedural-skill study does not change the durable Field Map.
