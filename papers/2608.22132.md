> **TL;DR.** A structured gap state routes among no retrieval, KG, templates, or both. The matched fixed-policy comparison supports source placement, but absolute correctness is low and most failures lie outside the Proxy.

## Research question

Should a biomedical agent retrieve graph facts, reusable templates, both, or neither as a function of its current reasoning gap?

## Research delta

SSE-Bio inserts a compact four-way Proxy after structured gap formation and before expensive evidence acquisition. Local template-field edits make retained procedural state inspectable, but they are separate from the retrieval-policy claim.

## Mechanism

`encode reasoning gap → select none/KG/template/both → retrieve → develop answer → critique → update state/template → stop`

## Evidence & attribution

| Claim | Evidence | Closest control | Assessment |

| Routing explains the end-to-end gain | large STELLA gap | interface/state/model package differs | **Not attributable** |
| More source capacity solves the task | oracle 18.06/13.05 | oracle both | Limited headroom |

The HLE audit assigns only 13.1% of failures to wrong Proxy choice; Dev reasoning and triplet retrieval dominate. Data construction executed 82.4K commercial-agent rollouts, but their cost is not reconciled with the reported 138 GPU-hour Proxy training.

## Where it fits

`structured gap state → source-conditioned routing → evidence acquisition`

This is an early signal for **structured-gap dual-source routing**. It does not establish a general self-evolving-agent trend.

## Open question

Can the routing increment reproduce with open models and a different manager/critic while matching evidence coverage, template inventory, calls, tokens, and latency—and while separately intervening on routing versus reasoning failures?
