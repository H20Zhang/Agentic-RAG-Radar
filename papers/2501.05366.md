## Problem

Long reasoning traces can encounter factual gaps after several steps. Retrieving everything up front is inefficient, but injecting large raw documents mid-reasoning can also introduce noise and derail the main trajectory.

## Core idea

Let the reasoning model trigger search on demand, then separate **reasoning over retrieved documents** from the main chain. Distilled evidence is reinjected so the original reasoning trajectory can continue with less retrieval noise.

## Agent loop

`Reason → detect gap → search → reason over documents → inject evidence → continue reasoning`

## Retrieval design

Retrieval is conditioned on the evolving reasoning state rather than executed once before generation. A separate evidence-processing step mediates what retrieved information enters the main trajectory.

## Compared to what

Static RAG, single-shot search-augmented reasoning, and iterative retrieval methods that do not explicitly separate document reasoning from the main reasoning chain.

## Evidence

The abstract reports experiments across complex science, mathematics, coding, and six open-domain QA benchmarks. The radar has not yet re-checked the full result tables or the contribution of trigger policy versus document-reasoning mediation.

## Why it matters

Search-o1 is an important design anchor because it moves retrieval **inside the reasoning control loop**. It also identifies a second systems problem: retrieval is not complete when documents arrive—the system needs a mechanism for deciding what evidence should be reintroduced into the active reasoning state.

That makes it a useful precursor to newer work on explicit evidence state and adaptive context construction.

## Limitations / questions

- How is a knowledge gap detected, and what are the costs of false-positive/false-negative search triggers?
- How much benefit comes from search timing versus the dedicated document-reasoning stage?
- Does evidence mediation lose details needed later in long trajectories?
- How do quality and cost change with matched search-call/token budgets?

**Curator take:** **4/5**. Important historically and conceptually because it makes search a native reasoning action; later work can be read as making its implicit state/context-management problem more explicit.
