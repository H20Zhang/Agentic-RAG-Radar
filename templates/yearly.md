# Agentic RAG Yearly — YYYY

> **Coverage:** YYYY-01-01 to YYYY-12-31 · **Status:** rolling/finalized

## Year thesis

State the strongest field-level conclusion that survives across months. A yearly thesis should be harder to change than a monthly thesis and should distinguish durable design shifts from short-lived framing.

## What changed from the start of the year

Compare the beginning- and end-of-year research map. Focus on abstraction boundaries, evaluation standards, and what researchers now treat as first-class system objects.

## Durable research shifts

Keep only shifts supported across multiple months or by unusually strong evidence. For each: **old default → new design point → evidence → remaining counterargument**.

## Papers that defined the year

Select only papers that materially changed the design space, evidence standard, benchmark, or research agenda. Prefer a short ranked set over completeness.

## Ideas that weakened or failed to generalize

Preserve negative results, failed trends, and claims that looked important mid-year but did not survive stronger evidence.

## Evidence audit

Summarize benchmark concentration, matched-budget quality, replication/open-source evidence, recurring confounders, and how much of the year's important work received full-text/ablation-level review.

## Open problems entering next year

List 3–7 questions whose resolution would materially change the field.

## Minimal yearly reading path

Give the smallest reading sequence that teaches the year's durable changes rather than replaying chronology.
