# Visual brief — Graph-R1

**Renderer:** GPT-image-gen  
**Status:** pending generation  
**Grounding:** abstract/project-level; full-paper verification pending

## Visual question

How does Graph-R1 turn graph retrieval from a fixed context-building step into a learned multi-turn policy?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- muted baseline: graph → one-shot/fixed retrieval → context → answer;
- agent reasoning state interacting with a graph/hypergraph environment;
- repeated graph retrieval actions and evidence observations;
- trajectory ending in final answer;
- outcome-level reward feeding policy optimization over the trajectory.

## Research delta to emphasize

**The graph is an interactive environment and the retrieval trajectory itself becomes the learning object.**

## Compared with

Traditional GraphRAG with fixed one-shot retrieval or graph-context construction outside the learned policy.

## Do not imply

- Do not invent specific graph actions or reward components beyond outcome-level RL unless full text confirms them.
- Do not imply intermediate credit assignment is solved.
- Do not put unverified benchmark gains in the image.

## Art direction

Wide learning diagram. Left: compact static GraphRAG pipe. Right: agent ↔ graph loop with 2–3 repeated actions/observations, ending in answer; a clean reward arrow flows backward to the trajectory/policy. The graph should be schematic, not decorative. Minimal labels: `graph action`, `evidence`, `reward`, `policy`.
