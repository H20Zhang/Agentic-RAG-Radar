# Visual grounding brief — Critic-R (2606.00590)

**Visual question:** How does natural-language dissatisfaction with retrieved evidence become both test-time repair and retriever training signal?

**Grounded mechanism:** `retrieve → reasoner reaction → critic sufficiency check`; if insufficient, `rewrite query/instruction → retrieve again`. Successful versus rejected refinement documents then become positive/hard-negative pairs for retriever training.

**Research delta:** A feedback bridge from the agent's introspective trace back into retrieval at both inference and training time.

**Compared with:** Frozen retrievers and co-trained agent/retriever systems that require different supervision.

**Do not imply:** Do not hide extra critic/retrieval compute; do not imply introspection is always truthful; do not conflate Critic-R-Zero and Critic-Embed contributions.

**Grounding status:** Full paper and official code repository checked 2026-08-14.

**Preferred grammar:** Feedback loop plus trajectory-to-training branch. Labels: `retrieve`, `reason`, `critic`, `rewrite`, `accept`, `train retriever`.
