# Visual grounding — The Compaction Cliff

**Visual question.** Why does uniform compaction erase safety-critical state, and where can type-aware retention intervene?

**Grounded mechanism.** A classifier assigns retained items to types. TypeCompact preserves classified constraints, TypeDecompose replicates in-scope rules across partitions, TypeRetrieve pins applicable constraints before relevance, and a guard verifier rejects missing constraints.

**Research delta.** Treat retention fidelity as type-dependent across compaction, decomposition, and retrieval rather than applying one lossy policy to every token.

**Compared with.** Type-blind compactors and score-only retrieval under shared artifact budgets; downstream context tokens are only partly matched.

**Do not imply.** The guarantee covers missed constraints; classifier cost is zero; typed metadata is available to every baseline; downstream performance is token-matched; or one paper's three operators are independent trend evidence.
