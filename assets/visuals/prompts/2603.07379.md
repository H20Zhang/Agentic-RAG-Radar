# Visual brief — Agentic RAG SoK

**Renderer:** GPT-image-gen  
**Status:** pending generation  
**Grounding:** abstract-level; full-paper coverage/novelty audit pending

## Visual question

What becomes clearer when Agentic RAG is organized as a sequential decision process rather than a list of RAG variants?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- state / partial information;
- control policy selecting retrieval/tool action;
- retrieval/tool observation;
- state update;
- repeat or stop/generate;
- lightweight surrounding labels showing architecture/evaluation/risk as views on the loop.

## Research delta to emphasize

**The organizing unit is the retrieval trajectory/control loop.** Taxonomy and evaluation should reason about state, action, observation, and stopping rather than only retriever or application labels.

## Compared with

RAG taxonomies organized primarily by components, retriever type, or application domain.

## Do not imply

- Do not imply the SoK introduces a new retrieval method or benchmark.
- Do not claim taxonomy novelty or literature completeness before full-paper comparison.
- Do not overcrowd the image with every survey category.

## Art direction

Clean central decision loop with four or five nodes, surrounded by three subtle lenses: `architecture`, `evaluation`, `risk`. The central loop should dominate. Minimal academic infographic style; no giant taxonomy tree and no decorative robot/network imagery.
