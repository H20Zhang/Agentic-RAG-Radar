# Visual brief — Search-o1

**Renderer:** GPT-image-gen  
**Status:** pending generation  
**Grounding:** abstract/project-level; full-paper verification pending

## Visual question

How does Search-o1 trigger search inside a reasoning trace while preventing raw retrieved documents from overwhelming the main chain?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- main reasoning trajectory;
- a detected knowledge gap / uncertainty point;
- external search triggered on demand;
- a separate `reason over documents` stage;
- distilled evidence returning to the main reasoning trajectory;
- continued reasoning toward the answer.

## Research delta to emphasize

**Search becomes a reasoning-time action, and retrieved evidence is mediated before re-entering the main chain.**

## Compared with

Static RAG executed before reasoning and iterative retrieval that injects raw documents directly into the main reasoning trace.

## Do not imply

- Do not invent the exact uncertainty detector or search trigger algorithm.
- Do not imply the separate document-reasoning step guarantees factuality.
- Do not put benchmark numbers into the image before full-paper verification.

## Art direction

Wide horizontal reasoning timeline. Most of the line is the main reasoning path; one visible `knowledge gap` branches downward to `search`, then `reason over docs`, then returns as a compact evidence packet to the main line. Keep the visual focused on the detour-and-reinjection pattern. Minimal labels, no decorative robot character.
