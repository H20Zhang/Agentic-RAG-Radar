# Visual brief — LLM-Wiki

**Status:** pending generation after QC-safe one-paper rendering

## Visual question

How does LLM-Wiki replace flat retrieval-as-lookup with agent-composable traversal over a compiled, linked knowledge surface?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- muted baseline: query → flat chunks/vector lookup → fixed context;
- compile-time transformation of source documents into Wiki pages with explicit links;
- query-time agent tools `search` and `read`;
- links discovered during reads becoming the next traversal target;
- evidence-sufficiency check deciding continue versus answer;
- Error Book shown only as a secondary maintenance loop improving the Wiki over ingestion batches.

## Research delta to emphasize

**The index product becomes an agent-facing environment.** Structure is compiled ahead of time, while search/read/link traversal lets observations change later retrieval steps.

## Compared with

Dense top-k RAG, summary-tree retrieval, and graph-enhanced indexes that do not expose the same human-readable traversal surface.

## Do not imply

- Do not imply `link-following` is a separate third API call; the paper exposes links through `wiki_read`, with search/read as the two concrete tools.
- Do not merge the Error Book maintenance loop with query-time reasoning.
- Do not claim web-scale validation; the public benchmark corpora are bounded subsets.
- Do not put benchmark numbers in the image.

## Art direction

Wide before/after diagram. Left: flat independent chunks entering one top-k box. Right: a small linked Wiki surface; `search → read → follow discovered link → read → sufficient?` forms the query-time loop. A subtle lower strip labeled `Error Book → repair` points back to the Wiki at index time. Minimal labels: `compile`, `search`, `read`, `links`, `sufficient?`, `repair`.
