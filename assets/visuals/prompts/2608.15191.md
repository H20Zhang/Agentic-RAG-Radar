# Visual brief — RAAC

**Renderer:** GPT-image-gen  
**Status:** pending  
**Grounding:** full-text checked

## Visual question

How do retrieval-aware progress signals turn a stagnant deep-research trajectory into a `continue`, `intervene`, or `stop` decision?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- an existing DRA emits search queries and receives top-k documents;
- four trajectory signals summarize criteria coverage, document novelty, query diversity, and alignment to the original question;
- RAAC observes the signals plus trajectory history;
- `continue` leaves the trajectory unchanged;
- `intervene` invokes a critical re-thinker that diagnoses stagnation and proposes a substantially different search query;
- `stop` ends search and triggers the final answer.

## Research delta to emphasize

**Make search progress observable and actionable, rather than letting a long trajectory continue after retrieval novelty/coverage has saturated.**

## Compared with

The same underlying DRA without RAAC.

## Do not imply

- Do not imply RAAC always reduces search calls; some agent/dataset cells increase calls.
- Do not equate fewer search calls with lower total compute; controller and re-thinker LLM calls add cost.
- Do not imply the four signals alone explain the gain; `intervene` bundles extra reasoning and query generation.
- Do not invent gold-document access at runtime.
- Do not place benchmark numbers or model names in the image.

## Art direction

Wide clean control-loop explainer. Use at most 7 short labels such as `search`, `coverage`, `novelty`, `continue`, `redirect`, `stop`, `answer`. Visually contrast a muted stagnant loop with the progress-aware branch. No dashboard UI.
