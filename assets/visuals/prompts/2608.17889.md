# Visual grounding — VisDocAgentBench

**Visual question.** Where does an agentic visual-document retriever fail between target discovery, page examination, evidence-role integration, and final ranking?

**Grounded mechanism.** Static and agentic systems share a top-10 opaque-page-ID endpoint. Agents search a visual or OCR route, inspect/OCR/crop only discovered pages, reformulate, and final-rank under a 12-step cap. The benchmark contains direct, one-bridge, and two-bridge paths; ablations remove iterative search, inspection, crops, route text, or provide support pages.

**Research delta.** Move evaluation from downstream QA or one-shot query-page matching to a ranked retrieval contract that exposes iterative acquisition failures.

**Compared with.** Static Qwen visual/OCR retrieval, BM25/RRF, Nemotron late interaction, and a GPT-5.6-sol fixed top-30 inspection control.

**Do not imply.** Agent policy is matched to the strongest static backend; more steps mean more capability; support injection measures discovery; or results generalize beyond the small fixed English scientific corpus.
