## Visual question

How does PlanRAG use an explicit plan to control iterative data acquisition?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- decision/task question;
- explicit high-level plan generated before retrieval;
- plan-conditioned data query/retrieval;
- retrieved data analysis updating reasoning;
- loop back to the plan/query stage until decision/answer.

## Research delta to emphasize

**Planning is upstream of retrieval.** The plan specifies what information should be acquired next instead of allowing retrieval to become an unstructured sequence of searches.

## Compared with

Single-step RAG and iterative RAG without an explicit information-acquisition plan.

## Do not imply

- Do not imply the planner is trained or optimized with RL unless verified.
- Do not imply the approach is general beyond the reported decision-QA/database setting.
- Do not add database schema/operator details not present in the radar record.

## Art direction

Wide clean diagram with a visible plan card above/left controlling a sequence of data-query → retrieve/analyze steps, then a final decision. Contrast with a small muted baseline that repeatedly searches without a plan. Minimal labels: `plan`, `data query`, `evidence`, `update`, `decision`.
