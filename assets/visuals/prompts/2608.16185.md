# Visual brief — LENS

**Renderer:** GPT-image-gen  
**Status:** pending  
**Grounding:** full-text checked

## Visual question

What changes when evidence is localized over dynamic raw documents **after the query arrives** instead of pre-materialized into fixed chunks and a persistent index?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- muted baseline: raw documents → fixed chunk/index materialization → retrieve pre-made units;
- LENS keeps raw documents as the source and treats candidate evidence regions as latent/query-dependent;
- a query-conditioned prior proposes candidate regions;
- complementary lexical/local/exploratory proposals select raw-document windows;
- an LLM relevance oracle observes raw text and updates evidence beliefs;
- the loop continues under a budget and consolidates source-grounded evidence before answering.

## Research delta to emphasize

**Move evidence materialization from preprocessing time to budgeted query-time localization over the raw source.**

## Compared with

Indexed RAG and an index-free ReAct-style search baseline on matched raw-corpus snapshots.

## Do not imply

- Do not imply LENS has higher answer EM than ReAct; D500 favors ReAct and fullwiki is nearly tied.
- Do not hide online cost; LENS uses more online tokens/latency in the reported controlled setting.
- Do not imply the multi-signal prior is necessary for answer EM; its fullwiki ablation is weak.
- Do not invent file parsers, graph edges, or a persistent index inside LENS.
- Do not put benchmark numbers in the image.

## Art direction

Wide before/after evidence-materialization diagram. Keep labels short: `raw docs`, `fixed chunks`, `query`, `propose`, `inspect`, `belief`, `evidence`. Make the query-time feedback loop dominant; no decorative database/server artwork.
