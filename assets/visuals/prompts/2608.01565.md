# Visual brief — DocNavRAG

**Renderer:** GPT-image-gen  
**Status:** pending generation  
**Grounding:** abstract-level; full-paper verification pending

## Visual question

How does DocNavRAG turn document structure and explicit evidence state into a retrieval control loop?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- a structured document environment with hierarchy plus cross-region links;
- agent/evidence state split into collected evidence and missing evidence;
- operations such as locate, navigate/expand, and fetch;
- retrieved evidence updating the evidence state;
- missing evidence driving the next navigation action;
- stop/answer when evidence is sufficient.

## Research delta to emphasize

**The retrieval environment and the policy are co-designed.** Instead of repeated global semantic top-k over flat chunks, the agent navigates document-native structure while carrying an explicit evidence-progress state.

## Compared with

Flat passage-level iterative RAG, repeated global semantic search, and fixed-traversal GraphRAG.

## Do not imply

- Do not invent specific graph edge types beyond hierarchy/cross-region relations verified by the radar record.
- Do not claim which individual component causes the reported gains until full-paper ablations are checked.
- Do not imply structure extraction is error-free or free.

## Art direction

Wide research explainer with a muted flat-chunk baseline on the left and a structured document landscape on the right. Make the evidence-state card visually central: `collected` and `missing` should be the only substantial text inside the image. Use a clear feedback loop from fetched evidence back to state. Keep labels minimal, no decorative robot character, no reproduction of the paper's original figure.
