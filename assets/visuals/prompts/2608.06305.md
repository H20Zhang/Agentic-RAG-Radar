# Visual brief — Beyond Top-K / READ

**Renderer:** GPT-image-gen  
**Status:** needs regeneration from legacy Mermaid  
**Grounding:** abstract-level; full-paper verification pending

## Visual question

How does READ change the retrieval interface compared with fixed dense top-k, and what does the BM25 result prevent us from claiming?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- left baseline: query → dense embedding/top-k chunks → answer;
- right method: agent state/decision → lexical search OR structural navigation OR bounded read → evidence/observation → update state → continue/answer;
- an annotation that the operation set is the research delta, not merely iteration;
- a small caution callout: BM25 ≈ READ statistically in the reported evaluation.

## Research delta to emphasize

**One opaque retrieval call becomes a small, interpretable operation space controlled over multiple steps.** The figure should make the interface change visually dominant.

## Compared with

Dense top-k RAG and the same iterative agent loop constrained to a top-k retrieval tool.

## Do not imply

- Do not imply READ universally beats BM25 or proves agentic control is necessary.
- Do not imply the evaluation is large or broadly representative.
- Do not invent internal MCP components, ranking models, or document parsing details not verified in the radar record.

## Art direction

Wide, clean research explainer. Left side muted and compact; right side larger and more expressive. Use simple document/search/navigation/read symbols, a visible feedback arrow into agent state, and at most 6 short text labels. No decorative robots, no 3D rendering, no paper-style figure imitation.
