# Visual grounding — Risk-Aware Reranking

**Visual question.** How does a tool shortlist become a pre-execution risk surface rather than a relevance-only ranking?

**Grounded mechanism.** A frozen ToolRet-BGE representation feeds a query-conditioned relevance head and a tool-level risk head; an inference-time coefficient trades the two scores. Optional audited rules cap labeled risk and permission exposure after reranking.

**Research delta.** Make candidate-action exposure measurable before execution and expose its relevance tradeoff.

**Compared with.** The same frozen representations and trained relevance head without the learned risk penalty.

**Do not imply.** Downstream agent safety improved; risk is query/argument/environment conditioned; graph smoothing is a safety win; audited filtering is free; or required high-risk tools are preserved.
