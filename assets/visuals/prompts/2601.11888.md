## Visual question

How does Agentic-R change retriever training from static query–passage relevance to trajectory-level utility inside multi-turn search?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- static baseline: query → relevance-trained retriever → passage;
- multi-turn agent trajectory: reason → query → retrieve → update state/query → repeat → answer;
- a training signal that combines local passage relevance with final answer utility;
- retriever updating as the agent's query distribution evolves.

## Research delta to emphasize

**The retriever is part of the agent policy stack, not a frozen similarity service.** Training should reflect how a retrieved passage affects the downstream trajectory.

## Compared with

Static single-turn relevance training, similarity-only retrievers, and agentic search with a frozen retriever.

## Do not imply

- Do not imply the exact optimization algorithm or reward decomposition beyond what is verified in the radar record.
- Do not imply the agent policy and retriever are necessarily trained fully end-to-end in one step.
- Do not add benchmark numbers until full-paper verification.

## Art direction

Wide trajectory-learning diagram. Left: a single query–retriever–passage arrow labeled `local relevance`. Right: a loop of evolving queries and evidence ending in an answer, with a return arrow from final utility toward retriever training labeled `trajectory utility`. Minimal labels and no decorative neural-network art.
