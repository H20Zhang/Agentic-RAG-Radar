## Visual question

How does SearchMaster turn self-play task generation itself into a controlled learning problem for evidence grounding, search depth, and tool-use quality?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- unlabeled seed documents in a local search environment;
- one shared trainable policy appears in two training-time roles: Proposer and Solver;
- Proposer builds an explicit cross-document evidence chain before generating the task;
- filters/frozen verifier reject invalid, shallow, or parametric-answerable tasks;
- Solver produces multiple search/open/find trajectories;
- correctness and minimum successful search depth contribute reward;
- an over-opening penalty discourages excessive open/search ratio;
- verified Proposer and Solver rollouts feed joint GRPO optimization;
- deployment is a search agent, not the whole self-play machinery.

## Research delta to emphasize

**The self-generated training distribution is regulated, not merely enlarged.** Evidence-chain grounding decides what tasks exist; search-depth reward decides which tasks remain challenging; opening regularization shapes which tool-use trajectories are reinforced.

## Compared with

Naive same-pipeline self-play that uses success rate as difficulty, plus prior search self-play without the same joint controls over evidence chains, search depth, and over-opening.

## Do not imply

- Do not imply the Proposer/Verifier are required at inference time.
- Do not imply search depth alone is the reward; correctness, filters, and OOP also matter.
- Do not imply SearchMaster eliminates training cost or verifier dependence.
- Do not present heterogeneous external leaderboard results as a controlled causal comparison.
- Do not include benchmark numbers, dates, citations, or paper IDs in the image.

## Art direction

Wide training-loop diagram with two semantic layers. Top/left: `seed → evidence chain → task → verify`. Bottom/right: `solver search → depth + correctness + opening control → GRPO → policy`, with a clear loop back to both proposer and solver roles. Minimal labels: `evidence chain`, `verify`, `search`, `depth`, `opening`, `GRPO`, `policy`.
