# Visual brief — ACE-GraphRAG

**Renderer:** GPT-image-gen  
**Status:** pending generation  
**Grounding:** abstract-level; full-paper verification pending

## Visual question

How does ACE-GraphRAG separate a rich hierarchical graph representation from an adaptive context-construction policy?

## Grounded mechanism

Allowed elements:

- hierarchical graph/context substrate;
- initial context or evidence view;
- a context-gap decision point;
- two complementary retrieval branches: depth-oriented factual evidence and breadth-oriented semantic evidence;
- evidence consolidation with provenance;
- adaptive policy/state feeding another retrieval decision or answer generation.

## Research delta to emphasize

**Representation is not retrieval policy.** The figure should show that a rich hierarchy can exist before the policy layer, and that the new contribution decides how to assemble context from it per query/topology.

## Compared with

Fixed-context hierarchical GraphRAG and non-adaptive context assembly.

## Do not imply

- Do not imply the adaptive policy is learned unless verified.
- Do not claim matched retrieval/token cost until full-paper checking.
- Do not add graph-building stages or internal ranking modules not present in the radar record.

## Art direction

Wide research explainer. Show a graph/hierarchy as the lower substrate and a small policy layer above it that can choose a narrow/deep branch or broad/semantic branch. The policy should visually dominate, not the graph artwork. Minimal labels such as `depth`, `breadth`, `context gap`, `consolidate`. No decorative network background and no reproduction of the paper figure.
