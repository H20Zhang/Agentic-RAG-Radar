## Meaningful research-map correction

Two distinctions are now explicit:

1. **Backend exposure ≠ agent inspection.** Pi-Serini shows that retriever family alone is not a controlled variable; tuning, surfaced ranking depth, and browse/read affordances can change the result, while deeper surfaced recall can remain unused by the agent.
2. **Environment retrieval state ≠ agent state.** SGR-Bench shows that external filters/scopes/views can be wrong even when the controller has reached the correct source; this should not be collapsed into evidence memory or rollback semantics.

The causal lens is therefore refined to:

`substrate × corpus boundary/interface resolution × environment retrieval state × harness/delivery × agent state × policy × realized resources × base model × training distribution × historical baseline`

Pi-Serini's backend tuning/depth are treated as subfactors of interface/resource matching rather than another top-level axis; the purpose is causal control, not taxonomy inflation.
