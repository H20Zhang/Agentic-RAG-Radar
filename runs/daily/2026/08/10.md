## Bootstrap anchors added

The initial design-space anchors were **PlanRAG, Search-o1, A-RAG, Graph-R1, Agentic-R, and an Agentic RAG SoK**. They are context, not claims that these are the field's six most important papers.

## Interpretation that changed the repo design

The strongest early signal was:

> **Agentic RAG should be analyzed as a control stack, not just a collection of stronger retrievers.**

The run exposed a recurring separation among **retrieval substrate → operation/interface → evidence state/controller → learning/evaluation**. This became the seed thesis for the weekly and rolling monthly compactions.

## Evidence caveat

The first-pass records were primarily metadata/abstract-grounded. `provenance.full_text_checked` remains explicit in canonical records; reported gains should not be reused as fully audited evidence until the research-reader pass checks tables, ablations, and retrieval/token budgets.
