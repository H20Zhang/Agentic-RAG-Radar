## Meaningful research-map correction

The prior `compile before retrieval ↔ adapt after evidence` tension remains, but W34 adds two hidden placement variables:

`evidence materialization time × state recoverability/reacquisition cost`.

The resulting systems question is broader:

`precompute/materialize/retain ↔ defer/localize/reacquire`.

Removing an index, a search call, or prompt state is not automatically an efficiency win; displaced work must be charged where it reappears.
