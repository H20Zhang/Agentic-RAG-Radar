## Meaningful research-map change

W33 adds **trajectory integrity** as a useful stress test without creating a new top-level taxonomy axis.

- State can be **editable/recoverable**, not only a descriptive record of sufficiency or missing evidence.
- Evaluation should test whether evidence identity remains coherent **across heterogeneous sources** in one executable trajectory, not only whether isolated tool or retrieval skills work.

The key caution is not to collapse these into one “memory” story: rollback semantics may not solve VAKRA-style entity/schema grounding failures.
