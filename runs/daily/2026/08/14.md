## Meaningful research-map correction

The previous radar over-compressed “retrieval interface” into A-RAG/LLM-Wiki/READ-era examples. The corrected 2026 lineage is now:

`top-k evidence delivery → raw DCI → bounded workspace → dynamic workspace → relevance-guided interaction`

The research tension is **compression versus control**, not lexical versus dense retrieval. Relevance can be a hard evidence bottleneck, a workspace constructor, or an execution prior; those are different interfaces with different scaling and causal properties.

The causal lens is therefore strengthened to:

`substrate × corpus boundary/interface resolution × harness/delivery × state × policy × realized resources × base model × historical baseline`
