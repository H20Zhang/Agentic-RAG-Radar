## Meaningful research-map correction

The new tension is **adaptivity placement**:

`compile before retrieval ↔ adapt after observing evidence`.

SIRA shows that some exploratory rounds can be removed when discriminative corpus information is observable before evidence retrieval. ReFind shows that when useful names/times/session context emerge only after search, result-conditioned iteration adds value. Therefore `number of search rounds` is not a stable capability metric.

The evaluation baseline also moves earlier to **evidence availability**. The training-protocol study shows that a positive reward can be spurious if the retrieval corpus lacks answer-bearing evidence and the model answers from parametric knowledge.

The causal lens is updated to:

`substrate/evidence coverage × pre-retrieval corpus observability × corpus boundary/interface resolution × environment retrieval state × harness/delivery × agent state × policy × realized resources × base model × training distribution/protocol × historical baseline`.

This is an attribution checklist, not a request to expand the taxonomy.
